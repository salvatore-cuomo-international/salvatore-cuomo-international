SERVE MORE. WASTE NOTHING.

来場者数が変わっても、届け切る。

  • 急増時：仕込みを待たず、在庫からすぐ提供At peaks: serve immediately without waiting for preparation
  • 減少時：余剰を別拠点・次回販売へつなぐIn slower periods: redirect surplus to another site or sales period
  • 販売実績を次の製造・補充へ反映Use sales results to guide production and restocking
